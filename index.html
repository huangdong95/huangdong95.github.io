I am a Ph.D. candidate in Financial Economics at the Yale School of Management.

My research interests include household finance, behavioral finance, and Fintech. My job market paper studies how firms can "shift the blame" and insulate themselves from customer relationship damage by delegating aggressive conduct, turning a canonical agency cost of delegation into part of its return. The paper uses the debt collection industry as an example and applies causal inference methods, large language models, and machine learning to study interactions between firms and their customers.

I hold a B.A. in Economics & Finance, a B.S. in Pure & Applied Mathematics, and a Master of Finance from Tsinghua University.

I'm currently on the 2026-27 academic job market.
